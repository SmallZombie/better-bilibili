# 隐私政策

**最后更新日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日**

Better Bilibili（以下简称「本应用」）尊重并保护用户的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明本应用如何处理用户信息，以及用户在使用本应用时享有的隐私权利。

## 1. 信息收集

本应用不会主动收集、记录或追踪用户的个人信息，也不会建立用于分析用户行为的遥测或统计系统。

本应用不会主动收集以下信息：

- 个人身份信息；
- 设备信息及硬件标识符；
- 使用记录及行为数据；
- 位置信息；
- 其他能够识别用户身份的个人信息。

## 2. 本地数据存储

本应用可能根据功能需要，在用户设备本地存储配置信息、缓存数据及其他运行所需的数据。

这些数据仅用于提供本应用的正常功能，不会由本应用主动上传至开发者运营的服务器。

对于 Bilibili 登录凭据等敏感认证信息，本应用会在本地进行加密存储，以降低敏感信息泄露的风险。相关信息不会主动上传至开发者运营的服务器。

用户可以通过应用内功能或手动删除相关本地数据。

## 3. 日志与错误报告

本应用不会在未经用户主动操作的情况下，将运行日志、错误日志或崩溃报告自动上传至开发者或第三方服务器。

## 4. 第三方服务

本应用作为 Bilibili 的第三方客户端，需要通过网络与 Bilibili 及其他相关服务进行通信，以提供视频播放、用户信息、评论、动态等功能。

此外，本应用可能访问 GitHub 等第三方服务，以获取版本信息、检查更新或提供相关资源。

需要注意：

- 本应用不会主动将用户个人信息出售、出租或共享给第三方。
- 网络请求可能包含目标服务正常运行所需的信息，例如 IP 地址、请求参数及必要的身份验证凭据。
- 第三方服务可能根据其自身的隐私政策处理相关请求数据。
- 本应用无法控制第三方服务对数据的处理方式。

用户在使用相关第三方服务时，应同时了解其隐私政策。

## 5. 数据安全

本应用不运营用于收集或存储用户个人信息的后台服务。

对于用户保存在本地设备上的数据，其安全性在一定程度上取决于用户设备本身的安全状况。

用户应妥善保管自己的账户凭据，避免将其泄露给他人。

## 6. 儿童隐私

本应用不会主动收集儿童的个人信息，也不会针对儿童建立个人信息收集或行为追踪机制。

## 7. 隐私政策的变更

随着本应用功能的更新或隐私相关要求的变化，本隐私政策可能进行相应调整。

如果隐私政策发生重大变更，我们将通过 GitHub 项目仓库发布更新后的政策。

建议用户定期查阅本页面，以了解最新的隐私政策。

## 8. 联系我们

如果你对本隐私政策或本应用的隐私保护措施有任何疑问，可以通过以下渠道联系开发者：

- GitHub 仓库：[https://github.com/SmallZombie/better-bilibili](https://github.com/SmallZombie/better-bilibili)

# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 2, 2026**

Better Bilibili (hereinafter referred to as "the Application") respects and protects user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Application handles user information and the privacy rights users have when using the Application.

## 1. Information Collection

The Application does not actively collect, record, or track users' personal information, nor does it implement telemetry or analytics systems for monitoring user behavior.

The Application does not actively collect the following information:

- Personally identifiable information;
- Device information or hardware identifiers;
- Usage records or behavioral data;
- Location information;
- Other information that could identify users.

## 2. Local Data Storage

The Application may store configuration settings, cached data, and other information required for its operation locally on the user's device.

Such data is used solely to provide the Application's intended functionality and is not actively uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

Sensitive authentication information, such as Bilibili login credentials, is encrypted and stored locally to reduce the risk of information disclosure. Such information is not actively uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

Users may delete relevant local data through the Application's features or manually.

## 3. Logs and Error Reports

The Application will not automatically upload runtime logs, error logs, or crash reports to servers operated by the developer or third parties without explicit user action.

## 4. Third-Party Services

As a third-party Bilibili client, the Application communicates with Bilibili and other relevant services over the network to provide features such as video playback, user information, comments, and activity feeds.

The Application may also access third-party services such as GitHub to retrieve version information, check for updates, or obtain relevant resources.

Please note:

- The Application does not actively sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.
- Network requests may contain information necessary for the target service to operate properly, such as IP addresses, request parameters, and required authentication credentials.
- Third-party services may process such request data in accordance with their own privacy policies.
- The Application has no control over how third-party services process data.

Users should review the privacy policies of relevant third-party services when using them.

## 5. Data Security

The Application does not operate backend services for collecting or storing users' personal information.

The security of data stored locally on users' devices depends, to some extent, on the security of the devices themselves.

Users should keep their account credentials secure and avoid disclosing them to others.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Application does not actively collect children's personal information or establish systems for collecting personal information or tracking the behavior of children.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the Application's functionality or applicable privacy requirements.

If significant changes are made to this Privacy Policy, the updated version will be published in the GitHub project repository.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically to stay informed of the latest Privacy Policy.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Application's privacy practices, you may contact the developer through the following channel:

- GitHub Repository: [https://github.com/SmallZombie/better-bilibili](https://github.com/SmallZombie/better-bilibili)

---

**This Privacy Policy applies to the Better Bilibili desktop application and its related features.**
